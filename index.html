The workshop

A family workshop in an international scientific trade

Carolina (Karolina) Riegel Blaschka

Family papers and correspondence place Karolina within the workshop household and Rudolf’s working world. The surviving evidence does not support attributing model-making or scientific authorship to her.

Leopold Blaschka (1822–1895)

By the 1860s Leopold was producing glass models of marine invertebrates in Dresden for teaching and museum collections, building an international trade around forms difficult to preserve.

Rudolf Blaschka (1857–1939)

Rudolf joined the workshop in 1876. From 1890 the workshop worked exclusively on Harvard’s botanical commission, which Rudolf later carried forward alone.

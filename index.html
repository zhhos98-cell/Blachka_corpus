Case 001

1887 · Liverpool

Liverpool Museum: one order, several documentary lives

An 1887 workshop sequence follows a Liverpool order from manufacture to accounting and dispatch. More than a century later, surviving models reappear in exhibition and collection records.

11 Oct 1887

The Blaschka workshop told its British intermediary that work on “the models for Liverpool” would begin the following week.

23 Nov 1887

A workshop account separated 247 Marks for the Liverpool models from an additional packing estimate.

Dec 1887

One freight case, marked I.B. 268, was routed through M. O. W. Möller in Hamburg for onward delivery to Liverpool. The dispatch letter records an invoice total read in the project OCR as 268 Marks.

2017

A modern account described a trove of Blaschka sea anemones rediscovered in a drawer at World Museum Liverpool.

2019

A published collection image identified a World Museum Liverpool specimen as Blaschka catalogue no. 93.

What the chain closes

Manufacture, workshop accounting, a marked shipment, institutional destination, and modern physical survival can be connected at collection level.

What remains open

The original model count, Liverpool accession numbers, the receipt register, and the object-level bridge between case I.B. 268 and individual surviving models.

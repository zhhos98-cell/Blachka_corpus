Research bibliography

Chronological working bibliography

Literature on Leopold and Rudolf Blaschka, their zoological and botanical glass models, scientific use, distribution, collection histories, material analysis, conservation, and digital documentation.

Scope

1894–2025 · third expanded pass

Entries are arranged by publication year. The list combines modern scholarship with selected historical reception, museum literature, and other published records that help reconstruct how the Glass Flowers and marine models were described, exhibited, conserved, and rediscovered.

Discovery and cross-checking draw on HOLLIS and Harvard finding aids, German and union-library catalogues, university library catalogues, JSTOR, publisher and DOI records, and citation chaining through Google Scholar-indexed literature. Workshop correspondence, invoices, sales catalogues, museum registers, accession files, and other primary records are catalogued separately.

Working note

This is a growing research index

The next passes will continue catalogue-by-catalogue and citation-by-citation. HOLLIS Series VIII, “Publications Referencing Leopold and Rudolf Blaschka and Glass Flower Exhibit,” is being used as a discovery backbone alongside German and union-library catalogues, theses, exhibition catalogues, conservation literature, and forward/backward citation chaining. Duplicate editions and modern reprints will be collapsed unless they contain materially different content.

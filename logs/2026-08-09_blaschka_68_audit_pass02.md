# Blaschka surviving-collection census audit — pass 02

The census now has 23 current-proof nodes across the seed and first expansion files. This pass focused on two things: baseline-positive controls and school collections that could generate post-2017 deltas.

## Italian school breakthrough

A 2025 peer-reviewed conservation/archaeometry study physically examined five Blaschka models from two northern Italian high-school laboratories.

- Liceo Giovanni Prati, Trento: at least two current models were studied. The paper explicitly says the existence of the Trento models was already known through reference [3], which is Ruggiero and Larson's 2017 worldwide-collections publication. This allows `baseline_2017_match=yes` without waiting for full map pin extraction. The paper also connects the historical collection to the Deutsche Gymnasium in Trient and the Blaschka sales register, with a proposed September 1886 sale. Exact current school total remains unknown.
- Liceo Ugo Foscolo, Pavia: at least three current models were physically studied and conserved: Anthea cereus, Aurelia aurita and Corallium rubrum. The paper gives no manufacture/sale date and only proposes an Alberto De Eccher / A. Dall’Eco dealer route from a surviving label. Unlike Trento, it does not explicitly link Pavia to the 2017 worldwide-collection reference. This makes Pavia the strongest post-2017 delta candidate recovered so far, but not yet a proven baseline-negative row.

The asymmetry between Trento and Pavia is evidentially useful but not enough to call Pavia a 69th surviving collection. Full baseline comparison is still required.

## Irish baseline calibration

The 2016 survey of Irish Blaschka collections is now being used as a pre-2017 positive-control source.

- Trinity College Dublin is baseline-positive. The 2016 study found 55 models in the Zoology Department and 82 across TCD collections; current TCD pages still confirm the Zoological Museum collection, with a later museum-level formulation of nearly 60.
- University of Galway is baseline-positive. Two historic orders totaled 132 models; the current university museum page reports over 100 surviving models.
- Queen's University Belfast is a historical-only negative control. The 2016 survey recovered the Cunningham order and shipment correspondence but concluded the collection appeared no longer to exist. It stays outside the surviving census until an object or successor transfer is recovered.

## Current state

- Current-proof census nodes: 23.
- Explicit baseline-positive nodes now include Cornell, Derby, University of Wisconsin-Madison, University of Vienna, Australian Museum, Liceo Giovanni Prati Trento, Trinity College Dublin and University of Galway.
- Strongest unresolved delta candidate: Liceo Ugo Foscolo Pavia.
- Strong current nodes still awaiting baseline comparison include Leipzig, Gotha, Graz and Museum für Naturkunde Berlin.

The decisive dependency remains reconstruction of the 68 current pins from the 2017 Corning map. Until that row-level baseline is available, `baseline_2017_match=no` is not assigned solely from absence in the present deep backend or from later rediscovery language.

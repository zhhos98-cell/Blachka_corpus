Privacy

Privacy & data handling

A minimal account of what this research site currently collects, and how future email subscriptions are intended to be handled.

Version
8 Aug 2026

Current site

The project does not currently collect names, email addresses or other information through the website. The email subscription form is present only as a design placeholder and is disabled.

Email updates

Before email subscriptions are activated, this notice will be updated with the identity and contact details of the data controller and the mailing provider used to process subscriptions.

The planned subscription flow will collect only the email address and the information needed to record consent. The purpose will be to send occasional Blaschka Object Network updates. Subscription will be voluntary, consent-based and withdrawable at any time.

Retention and sharing

Subscription data will be kept only while it is needed for project updates or to honour an unsubscribe request. It will not be sold or used for unrelated marketing. Any mailing provider will be treated as a processor and identified here before collection begins.

Cookies and analytics

The project does not deploy analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical request information as part of providing the hosting service.

GitHub General Privacy Statement ↗

Your choices

When subscriptions are activated, subscribers will be able to withdraw consent and unsubscribe from future messages. The project will also provide a contact route for requests concerning access, correction or deletion of subscription data.

Changes

This page will be updated before any new personal-data collection begins, including activation of the email subscription service or the addition of analytics.

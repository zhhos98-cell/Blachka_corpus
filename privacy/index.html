Privacy

Privacy & data handling

The public site is deliberately low-data: no project analytics, advertising trackers or email mailing list are currently operated. This notice also covers information you choose to send directly to the project by email.

Version
10 Aug 2026

Controller and contact

For personal data processed directly for The Blaschka Object Network, the project controller contact is Haohao Zhang. Privacy, access, correction, deletion, objection and other data-protection enquiries can be sent to zhhos98@gmail.com.

What the website itself collects

The project does not run its own analytics, behavioural profiling, advertising trackers or marketing cookies. It does not use browser local storage for user profiles and does not operate an account system.

Searches entered into the site are handled in the browser and used to route or filter the public pages. The project does not intentionally transmit search terms to an analytics service.

Hosting and technical request data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical service and usage information, including information associated with web requests, under its own privacy terms. The project does not receive a project analytics dashboard from this site configuration.

GitHub General Privacy Statement ↗

External images and links

Some open-licence images are served directly by Wikimedia Commons or Museums Victoria. Loading such an image necessarily sends a technical request to that host, which can include an IP address, browser or device information and request timing. The site suppresses the page referrer for external images where feasible. Those organisations handle their own server-side processing under their respective privacy terms.

Following an external link takes you to another organisation’s website. Its privacy and cookie practices then apply.

Email correspondence

If you choose to email a correction, source lead, rights query or other message, the project receives the email address, message content and any information you include. The purpose is to read, respond to and, where appropriate, document the research or rights issue raised. The usual lawful basis is the project’s legitimate interest in operating and correcting the research site and responding to correspondence.

Project email is currently handled through Gmail/Google. Do not send sensitive personal data unless it is genuinely necessary for the issue you are raising.

Retention

Routine correspondence is reviewed for deletion after approximately 24 months from the last substantive exchange. A message may be retained longer where it forms part of a documented source correction, provenance trail, rights/takedown record, dispute or other research record for which continued retention is necessary. In those cases, only information relevant to that purpose should be retained.

Updates and direct marketing

The project does not currently operate an email newsletter or subscriber database. Public updates are available through the site’s RSS feed, which does not require an email address. An email mailing list will not be activated without first updating this notice, establishing an appropriate consent/withdrawal workflow, identifying the mailing provider and documenting retention and international-transfer arrangements.

Sharing and international processing

The project does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by service providers needed to deliver the site or correspondence, currently including GitHub for hosting and Google/Gmail for email. Those services may process data internationally under their own legal and transfer arrangements. Additional processors will be identified here before they are used for new personal-data collection.

Right to object

You may object at any time to processing based on the project’s legitimate interests. An objection can concern all relevant personal data or a particular use of it. Send the objection to zhhos98@gmail.com and identify the processing you want stopped. The project will consider the objection under the applicable data-protection rules and respond without undue delay.

If direct email marketing is introduced in future, an objection to direct marketing will be treated as an instruction to stop that marketing.

Your other data-protection rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights to access personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion or restriction, and receive portable data where the legal conditions for those rights are met. Where processing is based on consent, consent can be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing.

For UK data-protection concerns, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. ICO complaint information ↗

Changes

This notice will be reviewed before any material change in personal-data use, including an email mailing list, project analytics, user accounts or new tracking technology.

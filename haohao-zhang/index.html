Research

Haohao Zhang is a London-based historian of knowledge in Britain. His work follows the material life of knowledge: the paper traces left by household writing, experiments outside learned institutions, archival reuse, scientific objects and the infrastructures through which evidence travels.

The Blaschka Object Network extends that interest into nineteenth-century scientific models. It traces glass models through workshop records, orders, shipping cases, dealers, museum registers, conservation work and surviving objects while keeping unresolved joins visible.

The Blaschka Object Network

The project is researched, written and maintained by Haohao Zhang. Public case studies currently include Prague, the Field Museum and Geneva alongside collection histories from Liverpool, Auckland, Florence, Tufts, Michigan, Mexico City, Newcastle, Nottingham, Vassar and Milwaukee.

Workshop and published primary material

Texts by the Blaschkas and contemporary sales literature

1880 / published 1881 · workshop-authored

Rudolf Blaschka, “Die Nacktschnecken des Meeres,” Sitzungs-Berichte der naturwissenschaftlichen Gesellschaft Isis zu Dresden, Jahrgang 1880, 23–26.

A short zoological paper by Rudolf Blaschka. The 1881 Isis volume is digitised by BHL and the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek.

BHL volume ↗ · BSB / MDZ digitisation record ↗

1880 / published 1881 · workshop-authored

Rudolf Blaschka, “Hydroidquallen oder Craspedoten,” Sitzungs-Berichte der naturwissenschaftlichen Gesellschaft Isis zu Dresden, Jahrgang 1880, 45–49.

Rudolf’s account of hydromedusae, repeatedly cited in later Blaschka scholarship and useful for connecting observation, zoological literature and model-making.

BHL volume ↗ · BSB / MDZ digitisation record ↗

1878 · dealer catalogue

Henry A. Ward, Catalogue of Glass Models of Invertebrate Animals. Rochester, NY: H. A. Ward’s Natural Science Establishment.

North-American sales catalogue. Harvard MCZ identifies the 1878 catalogue as offering 630 invertebrate models; page images survive through HathiTrust.

Online Books / HathiTrust locator ↗ · Harvard MCZ catalogue context ↗

1888 · dealer catalogue

Henry A. Ward, Catalogue of Glass Models of Invertebrate Animals. Rochester, NY: Judson J. Withall.

The later catalogue is preserved in the Henry Augustus Ward Papers at the University of Rochester, reference A.W23. It remains a principal concordance for model numbers and species names.

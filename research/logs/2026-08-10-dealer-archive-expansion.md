# Dealer archive expansion — 10 August 2026

## Scope

Archive-first pass on the three documented Blaschka marine-model commercial routes: Henry Augustus Ward / Ward's Natural Science Establishment, Robert Damon / Robert Ferris Damon, and Václav Frič. The pass distinguishes a surviving dealer business archive from dealer correspondence dispersed into customer and museum archives. No receiving institution is counted as a dealer archive without a named record group, correspondence file, acquisition file or stable archival locator.

## Ward

The University of Rochester remains the core dealer archive: Henry Augustus Ward Papers, A.W23, and Ward's Natural Science Establishment Papers, D.231. Additional receiving-side dealer witnesses were added:

- Smithsonian Institution Archives, SIA RU000189, Box 139, Folders 1–3: Henry Augustus Ward correspondence, 1879–1895; the finding aid characterises the bulk as business transactions between the U.S. National Museum and Ward's Natural Science Establishment.
- Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv, BayHStA Zoologische Staatssammlung 127: *Erwerbungen bei Ward's Museum of Mineralogy, Geology & Zoology (Rochester)*, 1875.
- Existing project witnesses retained: Haast–Ward correspondence in the Alexander Turnbull Library and three 1885 Ward letters in the Cornell Comstock papers.

These files are dealer-network evidence until item-level Blaschka content is demonstrated.

## Damon

The distributed-archive model remains. Rakow workshop drafts and the Lieferungs-Buch provide the strongest Blaschka-specific business sequence; McGill, NHM, BayHStA, Morgan and Te Papa preserve customer-side or general dealer correspondence.

A catalogue correction was made after checking McGill's current item listings. An earlier project pass incorrectly associated `CA MUA MG1022-2-1-109-0007` with Robert Damon; McGill identifies that item as a 13 February 1877 letter from C. A. White. Verified Damon examples are:

- `CA MUA MG1022-2-1-135-0011`, Robert Damon to John William Dawson, 13 February 1879.
- `CA MUA MG1022-2-1-117-0019`, Robert Damon to John William Dawson, 25 October 1877.
- `CA MUA MG1022-2-1-136-0004`, Robert Damon to John William Dawson, 3 March 1879.

The McGill Damon authority record lists ten outgoing Damon letters dated 1877–1881 and one incoming letter dated 7 February 1881. Individual letter content still requires inspection before any is assigned to Blaschka sales.

## Frič

The distributed trail remains Rakow + Australian Museum + National Museum Prague + BayHStA + Madrid. BayHStA Zoologische Staatssammlung 113 is a dealer-specific Frič acquisition file (1864–1869, 1883). Madrid MNCN/CSIC `ACN0364/007`, Václav Frič to Ignacio Bolívar, 18 July 1885, is retained as same-period international dealer-business evidence; it concerns insects and is not a Blaschka transaction.

## Result

Canonical dealer register advanced to schema 1.2.0. The principal archival asymmetry remains analytically useful: Ward survives as a concentrated personal/corporate archive; Damon and Frič must currently be reconstructed as distributed business archives across workshop, customer and institutional record systems.

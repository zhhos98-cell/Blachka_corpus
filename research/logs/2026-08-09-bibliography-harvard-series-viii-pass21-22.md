# Bibliography expansion: Harvard Series VIII, passes 21–22

Date: 2026-08-09

## Scope guard

The public bibliography remains strictly Blaschka-focused. The Harvard Series VIII publication list is being treated as a discovery bibliography, not as an instruction to import every item. General museum reports, general botany articles, travel guides, short factoids, unrelated glass writing, poems and fiction are excluded unless the publication itself substantially treats Leopold or Rudolf Blaschka, their models, the Ware Collection, or a named Blaschka collection / conservation / exhibition history.

## Source recovered

A mounted copy of Harvard University Herbaria's 63-page external list, *Series VIII. Publications Referencing Leopold & Rudolf Blaschka and Glass Flower Exhibit* (`publications_referencing_blaschkas_1878-2007.pdf`), is now the main source for this pass. It gives authors, titles, publication venues and archive locations for material from the late nineteenth century through 2007. The list itself warns through the HOLLIS collection description that the bibliography may be incomplete and that 2008–2020 material is not represented.

OCR/transcription errors in the external list are not silently normalized. Dirty German titles, uncertain years, and generic obituary/news titles remain on hold until independently verified.

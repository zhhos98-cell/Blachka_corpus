# Bibliography expansion: direct Blaschka pass 16

Date: 2026-08-09

## Scope rule

The public bibliography remains a Blaschka bibliography. Admission requires the publication itself to be substantially about Leopold and/or Rudolf Blaschka, their glass models, a specific Blaschka collection, exhibition, conservation campaign, reconstruction, or scholarly interpretation. General glass technology and generic material-context literature remain outside the public bibliography.

## Discovery routes audited

This pass used three bounded citation chains rather than general glass-history searching:

1. *Kunstformen des Meeres* (Stadtmuseum Tübingen, 2006), including its chapter bibliography;
2. Blaschka collection/conservation bibliographies from Canterbury Museum and the Natural History Museum, London;
3. later scholarly bibliographies that preserve otherwise difficult-to-find Reiling publications.

Exact-title searches against the repository were run before promotion to reduce duplication.

## Deferred rather than guessed

The 2016 volume *Sea Creatures in Glass* also contains chapters by James Hanken and Jane Pickering and by Florian Huber. Their chapter titles are securely identified in the Smithsonian catalogue, but page spans have not yet been independently verified. They remain deferred rather than entering the public bibliography with invented pagination.

Nancy Marie Brown’s “Flowers Out of Glass” is also deferred. Penn State currently hosts the text and marks the page as last updated in 2015, while the 2006 Tübingen bibliography cites the item as 1999. The original publication date/issue should be resolved before promotion.

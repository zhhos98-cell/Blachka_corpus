# Bibliography expansion: German sourcebook and catalogue infrastructure, passes 23–25

Date: 2026-08-09

## Scope guard

The public bibliography remains strictly Blaschka-focused. German union catalogues, digitisation portals, museum bibliographies and exhibition catalogues are discovery/verification infrastructure only. General glass history, generic lampworking, scientific-glass manuals, zoological model literature, museum history and Haeckel/context literature do not enter the public bibliography unless the publication itself substantially treats Leopold or Rudolf Blaschka, the Blaschka workshop, the Glass Flowers, or a named Blaschka collection / conservation / exhibition case.

## Major sourcebook recovered

Karlheinz Wiegmann and Meike Niepelt, eds., *Kunstformen des Meeres: Zoologische Glasmodelle von Leopold und Rudolf Blaschka 1863–1890*, Tübinger Kataloge 74 (Stadtmuseum Tübingen, 2006) is the strongest German bibliographic hub recovered so far.

The Stadtmuseum Tübingen hosts the complete open catalogue PDF. Its contents combine direct Blaschka essays with a printed `Quellen- und Literaturverzeichnis` beginning on p. 145. The catalogue itself states that it brought together work on multiple aspects of the Blaschka objects in one publication. It is therefore being used as a sourcebook/reference-chain seed, not bulk-imported indiscriminately.

## KVK / KIT and MDZ role

KVK is useful as a metacatalogue rather than a corpus: it forwards one query to multiple German and international library catalogues and is especially useful for establishing edition/imprint/holding variants, rare catalogues, exhibition catalogues and grey literature. It does not maintain its own bibliographic database.

MDZ is a full-text/digitisation route rather than a bibliography. It should be used after title discovery to recover scans or search OCR of older German publications. No specific Blaschka MDZ hit has yet been promoted; generic `Blaschka` searches are contaminated by unrelated authors with that surname.

Practical sequence for the next German pass:

1. Tübingen / collection sourcebook reference-chain discovery.
2. Exact-title KVK / DNB / K10plus verification and edition control.
3. MDZ / SLUB / Google Books / HathiTrust / ZOBODAT / institutional repositories for digital copies.
4. Repository exact-title deduplication.
5. Public promotion only when the item itself is substantially Blaschka-focused.

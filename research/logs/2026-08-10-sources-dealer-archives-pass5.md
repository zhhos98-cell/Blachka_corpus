# Sources pass 5 — dealer archives

Date: 2026-08-10

## Question

Where do the business records of the three documented Blaschka marine-model dealers/distributors survive, and which archives can reconstruct dealer-side transactions?

## Result

The three documented commercial routes are Henry Augustus Ward / Ward's Natural Science Establishment in North America, Robert Damon / Robert Ferris Damon in Great Britain and Ireland, and Vaclav Fric in Austria-Hungary, with additional international resale by Ward and Fric.

The archival survival is strongly asymmetric.

### Ward — intact archive

University of Rochester RBSCP explicitly describes Henry Augustus Ward as a collection of record and holds both:

- Henry Augustus Ward Papers, 1840–1933, A.W23.
- Ward's Natural Science Establishment Papers, 1876–1988, D.231.

This is the principal intact dealer/corporate archive in the Blaschka distribution network. Corning's global-census work states that US customer sales were reconstructed from the Henry A. Ward papers.

### Damon — distributed archive

No dedicated Damon corporate fonds was located in this pass. Surviving business evidence is distributed across workshop-side and customer-side archives.

- Rakow Research Library: Blaschka business-letter drafts and delivery/order books; published NHM research identifies Box 13 drafts and Lieferungs-Buch material documenting the Damon agency, payments, packing and breakage.
- McGill University Archives, Dawson-Harrington Families Fonds: the Robert Damon authority record lists ten letters created by Damon dated 1877–1881 and one received letter. Examples include CA MUA MG1022-2-1-109-0007, MG1022-2-1-117-0019 and MG1022-2-1-136-0003.
- Natural History Museum Archives: Damon appears across Zoology and Palaeontology correspondence and acquisition records; examples include DF/ZOO/200/19/70, DF/ZOO/200/20/106-108 and Robert F. Damon item DF/PAL/100/20/11 (1884).
- Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv, Zoologische Staatssammlung 124: acquisitions from natural-history dealer Robert Damon, Weymouth, 1873.
- Te Papa, MU000147/007/0017: Robert Damon letter of 1 March 1889 concerning the Godfrey Collection.

These items prove a reconstructable dealer correspondence network. They are not individually assigned to Blaschka transactions until content is inspected.

### Frič — distributed archive

No dedicated V. Frič corporate fonds was located in this pass. The business trail is distributed, but direct Blaschka transactions survive.

- Rakow Research Library: workshop-side Frič correspondence, including the 8 November 1884 draft concerning a commercial shipment and four-model gift to the Prague museum.
- Australian Museum Archives: Trustee Minutes, Outward Letter Books and purchase-of-specimens correspondence reconstruct the 1879 instruction to Frič to obtain Blaschka glass invertebrates and three mixed consignments sent 1881–1883.
- National Museum Prague: accession evidence closes the 1884 Frič-mediated four-model gift; current National Museum research also documents the V. Frič business and the transfer of remaining natural-history stock to the museum when the firm ceased in 1959.
- Bayerisches Hauptstaatsarchiv, Zoologische Staatssammlung finding aid: dealer-specific acquisition file for V. Frič (Prague), exact Blaschka content pending inspection.
- NHM Archives DF/ZOO/252/1/2/52: V. Frič correspondence from 1905, useful only as post-Blaschka business context unless content supplies an earlier collection link.

## Methodological decision

Dealer archives are now separated from general institutional archives on the public Sources page. A dealer is represented by an intact business archive where one exists; otherwise the page explicitly maps dispersed correspondence. Object-holding institutions are excluded from the dealer layer unless a named dealer-facing record series or item has been identified.

A new machine-readable `sources/dealer-archive-register.json` records the three networks, archive-survival status, archival witnesses, locators, evidential guards and critical gaps.

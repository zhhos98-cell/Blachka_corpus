# Bibliography expansion: direct Blaschka passes 18–20

Date: 2026-08-09

## Scope guard

The public bibliography remains strictly Blaschka-focused. A record enters only when the publication itself is substantially about Leopold and/or Rudolf Blaschka, their glass models, a named Blaschka collection, exhibition, conservation/reconstruction project, or substantial reception/history. Generic glass history, lampworking manuals, scientific glass technology, and contextual materials remain excluded even when useful elsewhere in the project.

## Duplicate audit / exclusions in this run

Exact-title repository checks confirmed that several tempting formal sources were already present and therefore were not duplicated, including *Drawing upon Nature* as a book-level record, Chris Meechan’s “A Glass Menagerie,” the 2008 Dublin Congress papers, Sabine Hackethal’s “Kunstwerke für die Wissenschaft,” major Corning/Harvard conservation papers, and UCL’s “Of Gastropods and Glass.” Whitehouse’s 2007 invertebrate essay was added at chapter level because the chapter itself was absent even though the edited volume was present.

The Sheffield Elmfield glass-history bibliography remains a discovery aid only. No generic glassworking or material-context item from Elmfield was promoted into the public bibliography.

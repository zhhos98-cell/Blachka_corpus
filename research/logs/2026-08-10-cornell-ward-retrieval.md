# Cornell Ward procurement and custody retrieval — 10 August 2026

Cornell's receiving-side archive now resolves into several distinct documentary lives rather than one generic collection history.

The John Henry and Anna Botsford Comstock papers (21-23-25) list three Henry Augustus Ward letters in Box 2, Folder 7, dated 3, 8 and 23 October 1885. William Warmus later states that the 23 October letter refers to a shipment of glass models and also reports a 16 October 1882 Ward-to-President Andrew D. White letter about the glass models. The first claim has a stable archival folder but still requires manuscript inspection for content; the second currently lacks a stable Cornell archival reference and remains a retrieval lead.

Later custody is much more precisely catalogued. Elaine D. Engst papers (13-6-4088), Box 1 Folder 66, contains correspondence on the permanent loan of the Blaschka models to the Corning Museum of Glass (1963, 1990). Barlow Ware papers (4-3-2587), Box 4 Folder 18, is the 2006 Blaschka Marine Invertebrate Collection restoration-project binder; Folder 19 contains Blaschka collection material from 1993-2006. Albert R. Mann Library exhibition materials (36-1-3601), Box 3, includes the March-July 2003 Blaschka Glass Invertebrates exhibition.

The methodological point is to keep these strata separate: an 1880s procurement file, twentieth-century custody/loan paperwork, twenty-first-century restoration records and exhibition-making documents are different archival events. Later documentation may preserve copies or recollections of earlier transactions, but it is not automatically evidence for the original purchase.

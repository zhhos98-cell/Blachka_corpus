# Bibliography closure audit — pass 37

Date: 2026-08-09

Purpose: one final high-recall check after the multilingual/global pass 36. The aim was to catch substantial Blaschka-specific records that general English-language discovery tends to miss, while refusing to pad the public bibliography with derivative mentions or weakly verified translations.

## Added

1. Isabelle Pirotte and Valérie Rousseau, *Modèles en verre d’invertébrés marins de Léopold et Rudolf Blaschka : conservation préventive et proposition d’un traitement de restauration : collection de l’Institut de zoologie de l’Université de Liège*. Master’s thesis, Conservation-restauration des objets d’art, option céramique et verre, ESA Saint-Luc, 2007, 199 leaves. French. Verified through the ESA Saint-Luc Liège library catalogue. High value because it is collection-level conservation grey literature and predates the later Liège book.

2. 西野嘉章 [Yoshiaki Nishino], ジョージ・ラウドン [George Loudon], 秋篠宮眞子 [Mako Akishino], authors; 菊池敏正 [Toshimasa Kikuchi], 松原始 [Hajime Matsubara], 藏田愛子 [Aiko Kurata], eds., *十九世紀ミラビリア博物誌――ミスター・ラウドンの蒐集室より*. Intermediatheque Natural History Series 5. Tokyo: The University Museum, The University of Tokyo, 2019. Japanese. Bibliographic metadata verified through the University Museum / CiNii; the official Intermediatheque exhibition record states that fourteen rare Blaschka glass biological models were shown.

3. David Arnaud and Marco Saraceno, “Qui regarde la traduction métrique du geste ?” *Socio-anthropologie* 51 (2025): 189–194. French. DOI 10.4000/14av6. OpenEdition full text. High value because it documents the contemporary “Blaschka Renaissance” reconstruction of the Liège *Clava multicornis* model and the motion-capture / gesture-documentation problem, extending the bibliography from object conservation into craft-process reconstruction and intangible heritage.

## Duplicate audit

Repository searches were run before promotion. The 2007 Pirotte/Rousseau thesis, the 2019 Japanese exhibition catalogue, and the 2025 Arnaud/Saraceno article did not occur in bibliography passes 6–36. Several apparently promising multilingual hits were already present and therefore were not duplicated, including Rui Sasaki’s 2017 Japanese article, María Isabel Herráez Martín’s Spanish “Los modelos Blaschka,” Aurélie Michel’s 2010 French article, and the 2012 Liège volume *Blaschka : Maîtres et modèles*.

## Searches tested but not promoted

Portuguese: a September 2021 *National Geographic Portugal* translation/adaptation of Natasha Daly’s Guido Mocafico feature was located and bibliographically recoverable, but it is a translated magazine version of an already identifiable National Geographic feature rather than new Blaschka scholarship or collection evidence. It remains outside the public bibliography in this closure pass.

Polish / Russian: searches for local-language museum and scholarly records returned generic glass-history material or derivative Blaschka pages rather than substantial, independently useful Blaschka publications.

South Asia / Africa: searches were also run against historical/current museum contexts. They produced useful collection-history leads and historical purchaser references, but no sufficiently substantial local publication was recovered in this pass. Those are discovery/census leads, not bibliography records.

## Closure rule

The bibliography is frozen after pass 37 for this research phase. Future additions should come from a specific archival, institutional, object-level, conservation, or citation-chain discovery rather than another undirected global sweep. This keeps the page broad in language and geography without turning it into a list of every web mention of the Blaschkas.

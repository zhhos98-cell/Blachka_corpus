How to read it

Route, material and evidence stay separate.

A dated place can establish presence without proving a collecting trip. A corridor can narrow where observation remained possible without naming a mountain or milepoint. An object handoff can establish transfer without proving every intermediate mile. Passenger customs, bonded freight and museum inspection are therefore kept distinct. Return to Hosterwitz also did not mean that every specimen, reference book or modelling decision arrived at the same moment.

Rudolf Blaschka · 1892

A workshop on the move

Follow Rudolf’s route alongside the work done on the journey: observing, drawing, preserving plants, sending material and returning that work to the Hosterwitz workshop.

Drawings, specimens, seeds and instructions often kept moving after Rudolf left a place.

How to read it

Route, material and evidence stay separate.

A dated place can establish presence without proving a collecting trip. A timetable can narrow a travel window without proving the exact train. Historical landscape matches can identify a view while leaving Rudolf’s precise position open.

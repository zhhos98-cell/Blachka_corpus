About

Following Blaschka objects through records.

The project connects workshop records with later shipments, collections, conservation and surviving objects.

Project
2026

Scope

A case may begin with one model, an order, a shipping case or a museum group. The public site presents chains that can be followed usefully from the surviving evidence.

Evidence

Documented connections, useful comparisons and unresolved gaps remain separate. A shared catalogue number, nearby register number, similar form or later institutional succession does not by itself identify the same physical object.

If a claim depends on OCR, an incomplete register, a later recollection or a secondary account, that limitation stays visible.

People

People are included when a source gives them a role in an object history: making, commissioning, dealing, carrying, curating, conserving, supplying or researching. Qualified roles remain qualified.

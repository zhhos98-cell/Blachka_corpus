# Blaschka surviving-collection census audit — pass 03

The current-proof census now contains **34 nodes** across the seed and three expansion files. The purpose remains to reconstruct the 2017 Corning baseline of 68 surviving collections before assigning any definitive post-2017 `baseline_2017_match=no` status.

## Correction: Trento is a baseline conflict, not a clean baseline-positive row

The previous pass treated Liceo Giovanni Prati, Trento as baseline-positive because the 2025 conservation paper states that the existence of the Trento models was already known through reference [3], Ruggiero and Larson 2017. A newly recovered 2022 conference abstract by the same research group materially complicates that reading: it describes the five Trento/Pavia examples as unknown and states that both school nuclei had not been known and had not been identified as Blaschka production.

The safest reading is that the 2017 research universe may have known the historical Trient sale/location while the surviving physical school objects were only later recognized or attributed. Trento is therefore changed from `yes` to `conflict` until the 2017 map pin and current-status text can be read directly.

Pavia remains the strongest delta candidate. The 2022 abstract gives explicit post-2017 rediscovery/attribution language, while the 2025 paper physically documents three surviving models and gives no equivalent prior-worldwide-collection citation for Pavia. This is still insufficient to call Pavia the 69th collection without the baseline pin list.

- Kremsmünster Abbey: a recent collection-science count of 226 and a current abbey observatory collection page. Historical/current count conflicts remain source-layered.

The Humboldt University row must remain distinct from Museum für Naturkunde Berlin's 40-record OS001-02 historical-object collection. The two present Berlin custodial nodes derive from a related Franz Eilhard Schulze / Zoologisches Institut acquisition history but are now separate institutional collections.

## Baseline reconstruction status

Direct 2016/2017 positive controls remain useful, but the pin-level 68-row list is still the decisive dependency. The JSTOR issue page exposes a direct XML link for Ruggiero and Larson 2017, but the endpoint currently redirects back to the article landing page in this runtime rather than exposing the XML body. The Corning legacy map likewise renders current/original symbols without exposing pin text to ordinary indexing.

The next efficient passes are therefore:

1. keep looking for post-2017 rediscoveries in school and teaching collections, where the census is structurally most likely to have changed;
2. add strong current-proof rows from known pre-2017 institutional collections as calibration points;
3. continue targeted attempts to recover the Corning pin dataset or a source reproducing the institution-level map list;
4. maintain a hard distinction between `current proof`, `pre-2017 known`, and `baseline map match`.
